# Website v1.0.0

**Pushed:** 2026-11-15 20:13 (`0bcb14e`). Standard Parts page, PDF export, safer saves.

## New

- **Standard Parts page.** Bought items (Onshape Standard Content, vendor parts, items marked standard, and sub-assemblies marked standard) have their own page in the sidebar instead of appearing in Search Parts and on the BOMs page. Nothing in the database is changed; BOM pages, links and work orders still show these items. (`386f08a`)
- **Export a work order's parts to PDF.** **Export PDF** (next to "Showing N of M parts") makes a printable list of the parts the table shows, with the current filters and sort order: a large image, name, catalogue number and quantity for each. (`9241d72`)
- **Search Parts shows when it's loading or failed:** "Loading...", the actual error, or "No parts have been published yet", instead of always "Nothing found". (`141ff7d`)
- **Right-clicking a sub-assembly offers both "Go to Subassembly BOM" and "Show Part Data".** One the team makes opens a read-only summary. (`6cf62f8`)

## Safer saves

- **Editing a cell on a BOM page saves only what you changed,** so it no longer undoes someone else's newer changes. Failed saves are listed per part and sent again with the next edit. (`ed00725`)
- **Saves report failures, and local testing no longer writes to production.** Saving a part shows "Saved ✓"; a failed cell save shows a "Change not saved" banner. (`e6e42e2`)
- **Invalid numbers are rejected instead of saved as 0.** (`7f00521`)
- **Part details lock only bought parts.** (`ea20ed2`)

## Fixes

- No stale data when switching quickly between BOMs or work orders. (`0802c28`)
- Right-click and avatar click on a BOM page open the right part, also when sorted, filtered or the Avatar column was moved. (`d79ff7d`, `7f95743`)
- BOM and work-order pages show the saved part name, even long ones. (`f197edb`)
- "Linked BOMs" in part details loads faster. (`46e1382`)
- The last column of every table fills the spare width. (`b62e82a`)

## Not tested yet

- The Standard Parts page against the real database, and the PDF export on the live site in browsers other than Chrome.
