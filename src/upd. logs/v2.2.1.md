# Website v2.2.1

**Pushed:** 2026-11-24. A small update (+0.0.1).

## New

- **Phones: parts as cards.** On a screen narrower than 640 px, the BOM and work-order pages show one card per part instead of the wide table: image, name, catalog number, a large ×quantity, and a "Bought" or "Sub-assembly" tag. Tapping a card opens the part card; a sub-assembly card also has "Open its BOM ›". (`f5b80fd`)
  - On work orders, each card has **Status and Priority** as coloured chips you can change, with the same choices and rules as the table (bought items: Untracked / To buy / Finished).
  - A **Table view / Card view** button (phones only) switches back to the table; the choice is remembered in the browser.
  - Copy list and Export PDF work from the cards; Export CSV is greyed out in card view.
  - Desktop doesn't change.

## Not tested yet

- Checked in a desktop browser narrowed to 390 px, not on a real phone.
