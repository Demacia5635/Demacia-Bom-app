# Website v1.1.0

**Pushed:** 2026-11-16 22:59 (`dd49849`). BOM page views.

- **Three views of a BOM:** a **View** switch above the table on a BOM page: **Single-level** (the default: sub-assemblies are rows, like parts), **Structured** (open a sub-assembly with ▶ to see its own parts) and **Flattened** (every sub-assembly already open, like the extension). Quantities are per parent, edits are kept when you switch views, and the browser remembers the view. (`cd99eb5`)
