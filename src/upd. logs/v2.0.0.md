# Website v2.0.0

**Pushed:** 2026-11-15, in parts between 15:40 and 20:13 (commits `e9ea512`–`0bcb14e`). The first official release.

## New

- **Standard Parts page.** Bought items (Onshape Standard Content, vendor parts, items marked standard, and sub-assemblies marked standard) have their own page in the sidebar instead of appearing in Search Parts and on the BOMs page.
  - An item counts as standard if any of these is saved for it: Standard Content, marked standard, a vendor, or engineer "Standard Part". A sub-assembly also counts if a standard part record has the same ID.
  - Parts saved only inside a bought sub-assembly count as standard too, unless they're also used in an assembly the team makes.
  - Nothing in the database is changed. BOM detail pages, links and work orders still show these items.
- **Export a work order's parts to PDF.** On a work order, **Export PDF** (next to "Showing N of M parts") makes a printable list of the parts the table shows, with the current filters and sort order: a large image, name, catalogue number and quantity for each. The header names the work order, assembly and active filters. Choose "Save as PDF" in the print dialog.
- **Search Parts lists sub-assemblies** with a "Sub-assembly" badge; clicking one opens its BOM page. Top assemblies aren't listed, and a sub-assembly marked standard appears once, as a part.
- **Search Parts shows when it's loading or failed:** "Loading…", the actual error, or "No parts have been published yet", instead of always "Nothing found".
- **Right-clicking a sub-assembly offers both "Go to Subassembly BOM" and "Show Part Data".** A standard sub-assembly opens its part details. One the team makes opens a read-only summary (image, name, catalog number, description, engineer, comments, Open Onshape button); it's edited in the extension.

## Safer saves

- **Editing a cell on a BOM page saves only what you changed.** Before, every edit re-saved all ten fields as they were when the page loaded, which could undo someone else's newer changes. Quick edits are sent together, failed saves are listed per part, and a failed part's changes are sent again with its next edit.
- **Saves report failures, and local testing no longer writes to production.** Saving a part, creating a work order and editing a cell all go through the shared request code. Saving a part shows "Saved ✓"; a failed cell save shows a "Change not saved" banner.
- **Invalid numbers are rejected instead of saved as 0.** Quantity and revision take whole numbers; other number cells take plain decimals with a dot (e.g. "1.5"). Anything else is rejected and the cell goes back to its previous value. An empty cell still means 0.
- **Part details lock only bought parts** (with a vendor, or Onshape Standard Content), the same parts the extension locks. Before, any part saved without a vendor field was locked.

## Fixes

- No stale data when switching quickly between BOMs or work orders.
- Right-click and avatar click on a BOM page open the right part, also when the table is sorted, filtered, collapsed, or the Avatar column was moved.
- BOM and work-order pages show the saved part name, even long ones or ones with an underscore (they showed as "Part JJD").
- "Linked BOMs" in part details loads faster.
- The last column of every table fills the spare width.
- No more ghost or multiplying rows in tables (the table code is shared with the extension).

## After deploying
