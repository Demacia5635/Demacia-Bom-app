# Website v3.1.0

**Unreleased.** Committed on `MASTER` (`cf8a95a`), not pushed yet.

## Changes

- **Items marked standard in the extension count as bought everywhere.** This only changes records from older publishes that have the "Standard part" mark but aren't saved as Standard Content: the website used to ignore the mark. New publishes save both. (`cf8a95a`)
  - **Create work order** skips their catalog number, material and engineer checks and adds them as "Purchased", not tracked.
  - **BOM page:** marked parts and sub-assemblies are locked and greyed out, with no Open Onshape link. Part details are locked too.
  - **Work orders:** their name, status and method are locked. They're counted under "Hide purchased & standard parts" and left out of CNC merge suggestions.
  - The "Standard part" checkbox itself is still only in the extension.

## Not tested yet

- Nothing has run in a real browser: how a marked part looks on a BOM page, creating a work order from a BOM that contains one, and how it shows there.
- Work orders made before this change may show such a part with a locked status that isn't "Untracked".
