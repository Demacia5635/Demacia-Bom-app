# Website v2.2.3

**Pushed:** 2026-11-24. A small update (+0.0.1).

## Changes

- **Revision is the server's number, read-only.** Parts show a whole number that the server raises when their data changes (server 5.2.0); it can't be typed any more. "—" for standard parts. (`ce364bd`)
  - On the part card, in the BOM table, in a **new Revision column in the work-order table** (after the catalog number), on the phone cards ("5635-25-100 · Rev 3 · Bought") and on `#/orphans`.
  - After saving a part, its new revision shows straight away.
  - Old hand-typed values (e.g. "B") show as they are until the revisions are started at 1 on the server.

## Not tested yet

- Checked with made-up data only, not against the real server.
