# Website v2.2.0

**Pushed:** 2026-11-17 22:52 (commits `420e89f`, `b53260a`). `package.json` still said 2.0.0 for this push; it says 2.2.0 from `ba7da34` on.

## New

- **Show part sizes.** A **Show size** checkbox on the BOM page (right of the View buttons) and on the work-order page adds a read-only **Size** column after Name, e.g. `25 × 8 × 8 mm`. The sizes come from Publish BOM in extension 2.2.0, so the column is empty for parts published before that, and for bought parts. Both pages share the checkbox, and the browser remembers it. (`b53260a`)
- **A column that's shown appears next to its neighbour,** not at the far right, even after you've dragged columns around. (`420e89f`)

## Before it's useful

- The server must be redeployed (server v2.2.0), and parts published again with extension 2.2.0.

## Not tested yet

- Not checked in a real browser: the checkbox layout in both themes, and the column with real sizes. Only the type check and the build pass.
