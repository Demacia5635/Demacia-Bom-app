Demacia BOM – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Demacia BOM is an internal tool of the Demacia 5635 robotics team. It keeps the team's bill of materials (BOM) for robot assemblies designed in Onshape: parts, sub-assemblies, quantities, and each part's thumbnail, STL and Parasolid files. It is used only by team members.

What the app accesses

  • Onshape: the Demacia BOM Chrome extension reads assembly structure, part properties, thumbnails and exported part files from Onshape documents the signed-in team member can already open, using that member's own Onshape session in the browser. It can write part properties back to those documents when the member edits them.
  • Google Drive: the app's server uses Google Drive access granted by the team's own Google account to store and read part thumbnails, STL files and Parasolid files in the team's Drive folders. It does not read or change any other files.

What the app stores

  • BOM records: part and assembly names, catalog numbers, descriptions, quantities, the responsible engineer's name, comments, Onshape document references, and the IDs of the files stored in Google Drive.
  • The files themselves are stored in the team's Google Drive.
  • The Chrome extension keeps a copy of recently opened assembly trees in the browser's local storage so they load faster. It stays on that computer.

What the app does not do

  • It does not sell, rent or share any data with third parties.
  • It does not use data for advertising or tracking.
  • It does not access Google data of anyone other than the team account that authorized it.

Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party apps & services.

Deleting data

Team members can delete BOM records and their Drive files on request. Revoking the app's Google access stops all further access to Google Drive.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue in the Demacia-Bom-app GitHub repository.
